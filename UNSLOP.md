# Unslop pass

Done with the unslop skill (by Damir Sokolovsky, @heydamir, CC BY 4.0), 25 November 2026.

## Audit

Slop level: light. Built against the signs from the start; the audit found a few honesty and composition problems.

- Copy: the hero said "eleven stylists" and the facts showed 11 while the template lists eight
- Stylists page opened with the newest stylists, so a visitor met the least experienced first
- Hero swatch strands tapered to sharp points and read as spikes rather than cut hair
- No uppercase tracked labels, pill badges, glows, gradient text, emoji, italic emphasis, hype words, looping motion or 700+ weights found on any page

## Direction

- Product: the site of a neighborhood hair salon, so clients can read the price list, choose a stylist and book
- Character: warm, exact about prices, quietly confident
- World: a colorist's station, a swatch ring, mirror light, a printed price card
- Moments: the swatch ring of 24 shades across the hero; the price menu set in aligned columns by level
- Signature: the small swatch chip that marks each service category (menu, jump links, gallery shades)

## What changed

- Copy: "eight stylists" and the 8 figure match the team in the data file
- Layout: the stylists page now runs Master, Senior, Stylist, New Talent
- Visuals: strands end in a blunt cut a few pixels wide instead of a point
- Checked at 360px on every page: no horizontal scroll (the price tables scroll inside their own box on small screens)
